The journey

How a case runs, step by step

For insurers with outbound travel policyholders, and corporates with staff travelling for work.

  1. 1
    A traveller falls ill or is injured

    They call your assistance line, and the case reaches our desk through HMS Hub, by API or by email.

    HMS Hub
  2. 2
    Coverage verified by ACE

    ACE, our adjudication engine, checks each case against the policy to confirm what is covered and what is not, before a guarantee is issued. Sentinel, our fraud, waste and abuse engine, follows close behind.

    ACE adjudication engine
  3. 3
    Admitted without paying a deposit

    We confirm the right hospital and issue a guarantee of payment, through our network or an IAG partner abroad.

    Network Access & Payment Guarantee
  4. 4
    Followed every day

    A named case manager and our doctors follow the stay, query what is not needed and update you daily.

    Case Management
  5. 5
    Moved if they need to be

    If the patient needs a higher level of care or to go home, we arrange the transfer and the medical escort.

    Evacuation & Repatriation
  6. 6
    Every bill checked before you pay

    Each line is checked against the tariff and the treatment given. Errors are fixed with the hospital, not with you.

    Cost Containment
  7. 7
    Paid locally, invoiced once

    We pay the hospital in its own currency and send you one invoice in yours.

    Cross-border Settlement
