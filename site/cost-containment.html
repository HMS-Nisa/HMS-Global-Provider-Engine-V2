What we do / Cost Containment

Cost Containment

Every bill checked before you pay it

We check each bill against the contract, the treatment actually given and what similar cases cost. When a bill is wrong, we fix it with the provider, not with you.

What you get

Line-by-line review

Medical review

Our doctors question admissions, tests and stays the diagnosis does not support.

Negotiation

No tariff? We negotiate the bill down before it is settled, in or out of network.
