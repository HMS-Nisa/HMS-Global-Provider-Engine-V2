The journey

How a case runs, step by step

For assistance companies and TPAs handling cases for the insurers who nominate them.

  1. 1
    A case lands on your desk

    Your client's member needs care in Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore or beyond. Send it to us through HMS Hub, by API or by email.

    HMS Hub
  2. 2
    Placed at the right facility

    We place the patient where the care fits and issue the guarantee of payment locally.

    Network Access & Payment Guarantee
  3. 3
    Managed locally

    Our case managers handle admission, translation and the family, and report back to you daily.

    Case Management
  4. 4
    Moved across islands and borders

    Air, sea and ground transfers with vetted operators and medical escorts.

    Evacuation & Repatriation
  5. 5
    Every bill screened by Sentinel

    Sentinel, our fraud, waste and abuse engine, flags overcharges, duplicate items and care the diagnosis does not support, before anyone pays.

    Sentinel FWA engine
  6. 6
    Bills contained, in or out of network

    We review every bill and negotiate where no tariff applies.

    Cost Containment
  7. 7
    Local payment, one settlement

    We pay providers in local currency and settle with you once, in the currency you choose.

    Cross-border Settlement
