Option 1 · Self-service

Your rules. Our rails.

Your adjudicators check cover, approve the case and issue the guarantee inside the Hub, using our tariffs and network. You decide. The platform does the work.

  • Your rules, our tariffs and network
  • Guarantees issued from your Hub account
  • Bill review results returned to your team

Option 2 · Managed

Our team. Your visibility.

Send the case and our 24/7 team takes it from there: verification, guarantee, monitoring, bill review and settlement. You see every step.

  • Our case managers and doctors
  • Guarantees issued by HealthMetrics
  • You approve exceptions, we handle the rest

Mix and match: run routine cases yourself and hand complex ones to us, case by case.
